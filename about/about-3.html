Biography

With over 7 years of experience in front-end development and UI/UX design, I have consistently demonstrated a passion for creating innovative solutions that elevate user experiences. Leading teams and collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders, I have been able to tackle complex design challenges and deliver high-quality, user-centered products. My experience spans various industries, allowing me to bring a versatile skill set that blends both design and development to every project.

I’ve worked across industries such as footwear, data security, and healthcare, honing my ability to balance functional requirements with intuitive design. From building responsive websites to creating user flows, I focus on delivering solutions that meet both user needs and business goals.

I’m passionate about solving design challenges creatively and efficiently, with a focus on empathy for users. Whether it's wireframing, prototyping, or final delivery, I enjoy refining ideas to create seamless, impactful experiences that are both meaningful and user-friendly.
